# Des Moines Metro: What Local Businesses Need (Sept 2026)

**Coverage:** Des Moines, West Des Moines, Ankeny, Urbandale, Johnston, Clive, Waukee, Altoona
**Goal:** Find where AI tools/apps, better websites, or Meta ads would make local businesses' daily work easier or more profitable, and which businesses to pitch first.

> **Method and limits:** This pass used web search only (news, Yelp/Booksy listing snippets, chamber and city sources). The network policy blocked direct page fetches, so no individual websites were audited and no phone numbers or emails were collected. Treat every lead below as a **signal to verify**, not a confirmed pain point. See "Next run" at the bottom for the full Apify scrape.

---

## 1. The market in five points

1. **The region is investing in AI, but small businesses need someone to set it up for them.** The Greater Des Moines Partnership's Regional Summit (Oct 8, 2026) has an AI keynote. The Partnership says its main question is how to help small and mid-sized businesses *understand and implement* new technology. Leaders in the Business Record's 2026 Trends survey called AI the top force this year and said it has to come with upskilling. Nationally, only about 7% of small businesses that use AI have fully integrated it. The rest are experimenting. **Implication:** don't sell "AI." Sell a finished workflow that saves hours.
2. **Hiring is the daily pain.** There were roughly 970–1,000 open restaurant jobs in Des Moines in 2026. Nationally, 48% of restaurants have cut hours and 32% have closed on days they used to open. Growing sales and hiring or keeping staff are the top two problems small businesses report. **Implication:** automation that replaces front-desk and phone work (AI receptionist, online ordering and booking, auto-replies) is the easiest to justify.
3. **Businesses spend on ads but can't tell if they work.** 59% of small businesses say they see no ROI from social media. The usual cause is a small budget spread across too many audiences and creatives. **Implication:** sell Meta ads as one offer, one audience, tracked leads, and a monthly report showing cost per lead. Don't sell "social media management."
4. **AI search visibility is a new gap.** Customers now ask ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews to "recommend a plumber in Ankeny," and the answers change from search to search. Most local businesses haven't optimized for this. **Implication:** an "AI visibility audit" is a free, concrete way to open a conversation.
5. **Downtown foot traffic is weak, but the suburbs are growing.** Downtown has about 32 vacant storefronts (around 200k sq ft). The city started a $7M "Restore the Core" matching-grant program in June 2026 because hybrid work left downtown at about 60% traffic. Meanwhile Ankeny, Waukee (Kettlestone), West Des Moines (Westown/Jordan Creek), and Altoona are adding a lot of new development. **Implication:** downtown shops need paid local ads to bring people in. New suburban openings need a launch package (site, Google profile, Meta launch ads).

## 2. Needs by industry → what to sell

| Industry | Daily pain (what they'd say) | Best-fit fix | Price guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Restaurants / cafés** | Short-staffed, phones ring during rush, catering inquiries get lost, slow nights | AI phone/text ordering and catering intake, Google profile and menu cleanup, Meta ads for slow nights and events | $1–3k setup + $500–1.5k/mo ads |
| **HVAC / plumbing / electrical** | Missed calls = lost jobs ("said they'd follow up but never did"), after-hours calls, seasonal swings | AI receptionist + missed-call text-back, online booking, review-request automation, Meta ads for seasonal tune-ups | $1.5–4k setup + $300–800/mo |
| **Lawn / landscaping / snow** | Quote requests pile up, seasonal crew churn, fall cleanup and snow contracts need filling now | Instant-quote form + AI follow-up, Meta lead ads for **fall cleanup / snow contracts** (timely right now) | $1–2.5k + ad mgmt |
| **Salons / barbers / spas** | No-shows, texts at all hours, "hard to get in" (seen in Blend & Bright snippet), booking app fees | AI booking/rebooking assistant, no-show reminders, Instagram/Reels content + Meta ads to fill open chairs | $500–1.5k + $300–600/mo |
| **Auto repair / used car lots** | Phone tag with customers, inventory not shown well online, low trust | Inventory feed → automated Meta catalog ads, AI chat for "is this still available," status-text updates | $2–6k/mo (dealers) |
| **Gyms / fitness studios** | Member churn, trial-to-member conversion, lots of competition (Power Life, Orangetheory, Pure Barre, Life Time) | Lead-to-trial funnel, AI follow-up texts, UGC-style Meta ads | $1–3k + ads |
| **Downtown retail / food hall tenants** | Foot traffic about 60% of pre-pandemic | Geo-targeted Meta ads (downtown workers, event nights), event landing pages, Google profile optimization | $500–2k/mo |
| **Any service business** | Invisible in ChatGPT/Google AI answers, outdated site | AI search (GEO) audit + site rebuild with schema, FAQs, reviews | $2–5k |

## 4. Outreach templates (fill in the specific detail)

**DM, new opening (Mama Africa / Home Sweet Cone / Breakfast Club):**
> Congrats on [the new spot at 700 Locust / the Ingersoll opening].
> New locations get the most attention in their first 30 days, and most of that happens on Instagram and Google Maps before anyone walks in.
> I put together a short launch plan for [location]: ad concept, Google profile checklist, and a text-to-order setup so the line doesn't depend on who's working the phone.
> Want me to send it over?

**DM, home services (HVAC / plumbing / lawn):**
> Quick one. I called three [HVAC] companies in [Ankeny] after hours last week, and only one picked up.
> Missed calls in [heating season] usually book with whoever answers first.
> I set up an assistant that answers, books the job, and texts you the details. I can show you a 2-minute demo using your own business name.
> Worth a look?

**DM, salon:**
> Saw a few reviews saying it's hard to get in, which means demand is there.
> Cancellations are the hidden leak: an open slot that nobody gets texted about.
> I can set up a waitlist that auto-fills cancellations by text. Want a quick demo?

**Follow-up cadence:** Day 3 (one stat or insight) → Day 7 (mini case or mockup) → Day 14 (polite close: "leaving this here if timing's better later").

## 5. Offers to package

1. **"Never Miss a Call" (home services):** AI receptionist + missed-call text-back + review requests. $1,500 setup + $300/mo.
2. **"Grand Opening Launch" (restaurants/retail):** site or landing page, Google profile, 30-day Meta launch campaign, text club. $2,500 flat + ad spend.
3. **"Fill the Chair" (salons/fitness):** booking/waitlist automation + monthly Reels + Meta ads. $750/mo.
4. **"AI Visibility Audit" (any business):** free foot-in-the-door. Show what ChatGPT/Gemini say when asked for their category in their suburb.
