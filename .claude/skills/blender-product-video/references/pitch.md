# The pitch (step 6)

Offer: **this video free → 4 new videos every month for $500.** 6 brands = $3,000/month.

## DM template (under 70 words)

> Hey [brand name]! I made this video of your [product] from one photo on your page. It's
> yours, free — use it for ads, your product page or reels. If you like it, I can make 4
> new ones every month for $500 (new scenes, seasonal drops, launches). No pressure at all,
> just thought it suited the brand. 🙌

Word-count check before sending: must be ≤ 70. Personalise one detail (a recent drop,
a scent name, a colour).

## Reply: "How much for just one?"

> Totally fair! A single video is $200. The monthly plan works out to $125 each, and you
> get a fresh one every week without having to ask — but happy to start with one and
> go from there.

(Keep the single price clearly above the per-video monthly price so the plan is the
obvious deal. Adjust the numbers if the user has their own pricing.)

## Rules
- Only DM brands whose product photos you used — and send them their own video.
- Don't claim results you haven't seen ("this will double your sales").
- Follow up once after 3–4 days if no reply; then stop.
