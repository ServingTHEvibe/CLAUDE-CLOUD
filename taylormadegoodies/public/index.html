How do I place an order?

Open the Goodie Concierge and answer a few quick questions. Your request goes straight to Taylor Made Goodies, and we reach out to confirm details, pricing and timing.

How far ahead should I order?

For custom work, the earlier the better. Put your date in the concierge and we'll let you know if it's available.

Do you offer delivery?

Pick pickup or delivery in the concierge and we'll confirm what's possible for your date and location.

Can you match my theme or colors?

Share your theme and colors, and upload reference photos in the concierge. The more we see, the closer we get.

What about allergies?

Mention any allergies or dietary needs in the notes step, and we'll talk it through with you before anything is made.
